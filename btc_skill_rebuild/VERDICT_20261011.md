## Incident that the redesign must address

At 18:45 MSK on 11 October BTC was roughly 83,426. The old diagnostic 1h and 4h upper-first outputs were 35.7% and 31.8% while taker buying was around 61.8% of spot volume. Both upper barriers were breached minutes later and price reached approximately 84,002 at 19:56 MSK. The old targets were first-passage barriers, NOT predictions of a maximum price. The user's actual question was about a sustained rise, future price and whether to buy the OTC BTC core. Those targets did not answer it.

A precision-recall statistic on overlapping five-minute windows is NOT equivalent to win rate or profit. The rarity of these large events and correlation between adjacent labels matter. These are research numbers only.

At **18:45 MSK, 11 October**, even the amended one-hour model assigned only **4.54%** to a +0.7% first-passage event (spot-only 2.73%) which then occurred. **The new classifier still MISSED the exact requested warning**. Retrospectively designing a threshold to catch that one episode would be overfitting, not proof.
